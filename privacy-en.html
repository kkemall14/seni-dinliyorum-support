Privacy policy

Scope and contact

Seni Dinliyorum organizes daily tasks, notes and personal memory entries. Privacy and support contact: Resect@windowslive.com. This policy is prepared for the iOS version. Last updated: 28 October 2026.

On-device entries

Notes, reminders, routines, habit logs, people, birthdays, addresses, clothing/belongings, game results and preferences are stored in the app’s on-device space. Photos are optional. Personal logs such as water entries may contain sensitive information; these entries are not sent to a developer server.

Passwords and PIN

Password-section entries use iOS Keychain. An optional PIN restricts access to that section inside the app; it does not mean that every ordinary note is separately encrypted. You can save hints or choose not to use this section.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice input requires microphone and speech recognition permission. Depending on your device, language and Apple settings, iOS speech recognition may process audio on Apple servers. We do not claim that all speech processing always stays on your device. The app does not send audio to a developer server or keep audio recording files. You can type without granting these permissions. Apple’s speech and privacy terms also apply.

Other permissions

Notification permission is used for reminders. With calendar permission, the app displays phone calendar events without creating, modifying or deleting them. Camera/photo access is used for optional photos you add to people or clothing entries. People you enter in the app are not an automatic import of your phone contacts.

Password-protected exports and iOS backups

An optional .sdy export is encrypted with a password you choose. In Files, you can choose a local location or your own provider such as iCloud Drive. The developer does not receive the backup or its password and cannot recover a forgotten password. Exported files exist independently of the app. Apple device/iCloud backups depend on your account and system settings; we do not promise that app data can never leave the device under any circumstances.

Advertising, accounts and sharing

The app does not require a user account, advertising identifier or advertising tracking. This version has no developer analytics/personal-record collection server or advertising SDK. Personal entries are not sold. Apple’s system speech service and file providers you choose operate under their own terms.

Retention and deletion

You can delete entries in the app. Removing the app removes its on-device app space, but iOS Keychain items and exported backups may persist under system/provider behavior. Delete password entries in the app and exported files from the location where you saved them. Manage Apple device backups and file-provider copies through their respective settings.

Support requests

If you email support, your email address and the content you provide are processed by the mail service to answer your request. Do not include passwords, PINs or password-protected backup files. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address.

Children and health

The app is not specifically directed at children. Water and similar numeric logs are for personal organization, not diagnosis, treatment or medical advice. This iOS version has no HealthKit integration.

This website

This static support/privacy site cannot access your app records and contains no forms, advertising or analytics scripts. If hosted on GitHub Pages, standard connection information may be processed by GitHub under its own privacy terms.

Holidays, widgets and sharing

Holiday dates use the selected country code, optional subdivision filter and year. Only country and year are requested from Nager.Date; the service can see the connection IP address. Tasks, people, calendar entries and audio are not included. Dates are cached on-device; coverage and freshness depend on the data source. Widget snapshots and shared text stay in the app’s iOS App Group. Shared content does not create a task before user review and confirmation in the app; queued shares older than 24 hours are discarded when the queue is accessed.
